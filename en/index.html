Questions

What you will want to know first

What does a health check include?

It depends on the package. Every package includes your biological age, a full blood panel and a personal report. Plus and Elite add VO2max, a hormone panel, MRI and a genetic profile, among other things.

Who performs the diagnostics?

Aevia does not run its own clinic. We coordinate your diagnostics through a selected network of accredited laboratories, imaging centres and specialist physicians, and gather everything into one report.

How is my health data handled?

Your data is sensitive personal information under GDPR Article 9. It is stored encrypted, never shared with your employer without consent, and company reports are always anonymised and aggregated.

How quickly do I receive my report?

Within 10 working days of your final test. The report is always reviewed with you in a follow-up one-to-one consultation.

What if you find something serious?

You are referred immediately to the relevant specialist in our network, and we coordinate the further investigation. You are never left alone with a result.
