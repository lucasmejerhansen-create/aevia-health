Biological age test

Read more →
What is it?

Your biological age shows how fast your body is actually ageing, independent of your birthday. It is calculated from a broad set of biomarkers in your blood.

Why does it matter?

Two people aged 50 can differ by 15 years in biological age. It is the one health KPI you can actively move, and it responds in months, not years.

What actually happens?
  • Calculated from a full blood panel and clinical data
  • Compared against your chronological age
  • Included in every package
  • Re-tests with Elite and the annual agreement show the trend

Outcome: one number that tells you whether your body is ahead or behind, and what to do about it.

Included in every package

Full blood panel, 70+ markers

What is it?

A full blood panel analyses 70+ markers, from hormones and inflammation to vitamin status and metabolism. It is a precise snapshot of your internal state.

Why does it matter?

Most leaders only notice problems once they can feel them. The blood panel reveals imbalances long before symptoms, so you can act proactively rather than reactively.

What actually happens?
  • Blood draw at an accredited partner lab within 48 hours
  • 70+ markers incl. inflammation, lipids, glucose and vitamins
  • Report ready within 10 working days
  • Personal review with a health specialist

Outcome: you see what is happening beneath the surface before it becomes a problem.

Included in every package

VO2max test

Read more →
What is it?

VO2max measures how efficiently your body takes up and uses oxygen under load. It is the single strongest measure of your fitness and endurance.

Why does it matter?

Few markers predict longevity as precisely as VO2max, and it can be trained. It also drives your energy and mental clarity through a long working day.

What actually happens?
  • Maximal oxygen-uptake test at a sports-physiology lab
  • Measurement of fitness, thresholds and recovery capacity
  • A concrete training protocol built on your number
  • Included in Plus and Elite

Outcome: you know exactly where your endurance stands and how to raise it.

Included in the Plus package

Hormone panel

Read more →
What is it?

The hormone panel maps the signalling molecules that govern energy, sleep, mood, muscle mass and recovery, including testosterone, cortisol and thyroid hormones.

Why does it matter?

Hormonal imbalances often hit exactly what a leader runs on: focus, drive and headroom. They are invisible on a standard check-up, but measurable here.

What actually happens?
  • Extended hormone profile via blood sample
  • Assessment of stress, sex and thyroid hormones
  • Interpretation and recommendations from a specialist physician
  • Included in Plus and Elite

Outcome: you understand why your energy fluctuates and what can be corrected.

Included in the Plus package

MRI / whole-body scan

Read more →
What is it?

A whole-body MRI scans the body's organs and tissue without radiation. It looks for abnormalities that do not yet cause symptoms.

Why does it matter?

It is the most thorough screening we offer. For many leaders, knowing that nothing has been overlooked is the value in itself.

What actually happens?
  • Whole-body MRI without contrast at a private imaging centre
  • Review by a consultant radiologist
  • Any findings are coordinated straight to the relevant specialist
  • Included in Elite

Outcome: the deepest insight into your body, without guesswork.

Included in the Elite package

Genetic profile

Read more →
What is it?

The genetic profile maps the hereditary factors that influence your disease risk, your metabolism and your response to training and diet.

Why does it matter?

Your genes do not change, but what you do with them does. The profile shows where you have an innate advantage and where you need extra attention.

What actually happens?
  • Analysis of relevant risk genes and carrier status
  • Insight into metabolism and response to diet and training
  • Interpretation set in the context of your other results
  • Included in Elite

Outcome: you know your starting point and can play the cards you hold.

Included in the Elite package

Team report

Read more →
What is it?

The team report gathers the leadership group's results into one anonymised overview. The company sees patterns and risk at group level, never individual data.

Why does it matter?

It turns health into a measurable management tool. You can document risk, prioritise initiatives and track progress quarter by quarter.

What actually happens?
  • An aggregated, anonymised health picture of the group
  • Breakdown by risk category and key figures
  • GDPR Article 9-compliant anonymisation
  • Included in the company package and annual agreement

Outcome: a data-driven picture of your most important asset, without violating privacy.

Included in the company package

Quarterly re-tests

What is it?

With quarterly re-tests we measure your key people four times a year and show the trend over time rather than a single snapshot.

Why does it matter?

Health is a trend, not a snapshot. Ongoing measurement catches changes early and documents the effect of the initiatives you invest in.

What actually happens?

  • A trend report showing development over time
  • Up to 20 key employees
  • Included in the annual agreement

Outcome: you see the trajectory, not just the position, and can correct in time.
