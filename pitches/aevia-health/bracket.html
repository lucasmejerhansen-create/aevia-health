Elimineret af red-team

—To aldre (Two Ages)spektakel/stuntred-team: Three disqualifying issues as written. (1) Contradicts Aevia's own product: the engine (lib/aevia-engine) mandates that biologisk alder ALWAYS ships with a confidenceInterval + biologicalAgeDisclaimer behind a non-skippable doctor gate, and is not yet clinically validated (pending Judit Kolovics). The stunt's whole mechanic is two naked giant numbers with the gap as the message — exactly the output the codebase forbids. (2) Marketing-law risk is severe, not mitigated by typography: Danish markedsføringslov + health-service marketing rules judge net impression on a layperson, not restraint. 'ALDER I BLODET — 53' in gold beside a real face reads as a definitive clinical result despite an 'estimat' label; '+6 år at hente tilbage' is an implied/guaranteed health-outcome claim (must be cut); tagline 'Hvilken stoler du på?' tells viewers to trust the measurement over their real age — opposite of non-diagnostic. (3) GDPR art. 9: broadcasting a named person's health metric live on a public square + filming bystanders makes 'freely given' consent fragile (Datatilsynet exposure); next-day anonymisation does not cure the live moment. Plus: a 10-min square 'måling' is almost certainly fingerprick, not the venous blodprøve the validated, doctor-gated pipeline assumes — so the displayed number isn't even produced by Aevia's own clinical controls. Salvageable only by: showing ranges/the concept of a gap (never a live named second number), private written consent + private tablet delivery, killing '+6 år at hente' and 'Hvilken stoler du på?', wide-interval framing, and doctor-gating any bio-age figure — at which point it stops being a 'spectacle.'
